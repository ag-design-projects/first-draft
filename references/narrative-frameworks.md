# Narrative Frameworks

Use this reference to propose storyboard options. Select frameworks based on the uploaded research, audience, and decision moment.

## Consulting Structures

- Pyramid Principle: answer first, then 3-5 supporting arguments.
- SCQ: situation, complication, question, answer. Best for executive recommendations.
- MECE issue tree: break a problem into non-overlapping branches. Best for diagnostics and strategy.
- Ghost deck: storyboard the end deck before overbuilding analysis.
- 80/20: prioritize the few findings that drive the strongest decision.
- Market/strategy combinations: PESTEL, 5 Forces, 3C, SWOT, market sizing, profitability, M&A, pricing, transformation, and roadmap frameworks from `$consulting`.

## Design Storytelling Structures

These are distilled narrative ideas inspired by *Design Is Storytelling*; do not quote or copy the book.

- Narrative arc: opening context, rising tension, turning point, resolution, aftereffect.
- Hero journey: audience or customer starts with a constraint, meets friction, discovers a new path, and reaches a better state.
- Rule of threes: group the story into three memorable movements or proof pillars.
- Scenario planning: compare plausible futures and the choices each requires.
- Design fiction: make a future state tangible through a believable scene or prototype moment.
- Emotional journey: map how the audience should feel across the deck: clarity, concern, confidence, urgency, commitment.
- Persona: tell the story through a specific user, buyer, employee, or stakeholder.
- Co-creation: show how participants shape the outcome, useful for workshops or transformation work.
- Gaze and attention: guide the audience's eye intentionally with hierarchy, contrast, and pacing.
- Gestalt: group related ideas visually so the audience sees structure quickly.
- Affordance: make the next action obvious on decision, roadmap, and recommendation slides.
- Sensory pacing: alternate dense evidence with moments of pause, image, quote, or synthesis.

## Storyboard Options To Offer

Offer 2-4 options such as:

- Executive recommendation: SCQ plus Pyramid Principle.
- Diagnostic journey: issue tree plus evidence ladder.
- Customer/user journey: persona plus emotional arc.
- Future-back strategy: scenario planning plus design fiction.
- Transformation roadmap: current state, gap, initiatives, governance, milestones.
- Investor narrative: thesis, market proof, traction, model, ask.

For each option include: use case, tradeoff, slide outline, selected `layout_id` values, likely visuals/images, and evidence gaps.

## Layout-Aware Storyboarding

- Treat layout as part of the story, not a rendering detail.
- Pick layout + image treatment while proposing the storyboard.
- Use the selected brand catalog to avoid overfilled slides.
- If a story beat is too dense for a layout, split it before the user approves the storyboard.
- Alternate dense evidence slides with visual pause slides when the narrative needs breathing room.
